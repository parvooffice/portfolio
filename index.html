Coval — Multi-Agent Code Review

          02
          
EXP-002● LIVE

          
LearnScope — Watch a Network Learn

          
Trains neural networks in the browser on WebGPU and shows every weight, activation and gradient live. Rewind to any training step, and let a rule-based Model Doctor diagnose what broke. Includes a from-scratch autograd engine.

          
≈2,100img/s on MNIST96%test accuracy91tests

          
  • TypeScript
  • React 19
  • TensorFlow.js
  • WebGPU
  • Web Workers

          
live site ↗

        


        

          03
          
EXP-003◆ v1.0.0

          
FinLab — Personal Finance & Strategy Lab

          
Honest projections for students and young professionals in India. Compare SIPs, FDs, stocks and PPF after tax and inflation, test whether a goal is reachable across possible market paths, and backtest trading ideas with walk-forward testing.

          
10,000market paths per run4planning views

          
  • Next.js
  • Express
  • FastAPI
  • Supabase
  • NumPy
  • pandas

          
v1.0.0 release ↗repo ↗
